This site contains LLM-friendly companion files for articles published in the *Journal of Economic Perspectives*. Each article has an `.xhtml` file that is both human and machine readable.
 
To view articles, navigate to the [View Articles]({{ site.baseurl }}/view-articles.html) tab and find the corresponding volume, number, and year.
 
To guide an AI model on how to fetch all information in this site, navigate to the [AI Access]({{ site.baseurl }}/ai-access.html) tab, and follow the instructions to prevent access error issues. We recommend you point your AI model to this index of all relevant URLs and article information before asking a question:

```
https://github.com/liegroup-dartmouth/working-aea-jep/blob/gh-pages/papers/index.md
```

To download articles, navigate to the [Download Articles]({{ site.baseurl }}/download-articles.html) tab, and follow the instructions for ensuring you are obtaining all images and utilities needed to view the article locally.
