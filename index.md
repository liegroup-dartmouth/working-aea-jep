# JEP LLM Companion Files

This site contains LLM-friendly companion files for articles published in the *Journal of Economic Perspectives*. Each article has an `.xhtml` file that is both human and machine readable.

To view articles, navigate to the [View Articles]({{ site.url }}{% view-articles.md %}) tab and find an article of the JEP.

To download articles, navigate to the [Download Articles]({{ site.url }}{% download-articles.md %}) tab, and follow the instructions for ensuring you are obtaining all images and utilities needed to view the article locally.

Note that some article figures may include datasets in CSV format including relevant data points.
